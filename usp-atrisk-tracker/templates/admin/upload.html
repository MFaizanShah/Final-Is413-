What a file should look like

One row per student and one column per assessment, with the weight in the column heading:

student_idQuiz 1 (10%)Assignment 1 (20%) /40Final Exam (50%)
S11000001831
S11000002622
  • A blank cell means not marked yet. 0, NS or ABS mean not submitted.
  • /40 in a heading means marks are out of 40; they are turned into percentages.
  • One row per mark (long layout) also works, as do Weight and Out of rows under the heading.
  • Past cohorts also need a final_grade (A+ to D, or Pass/Fail) or final_mark column.

Privacy: student IDs are replaced with codes when you save. Names, emails and other personal columns are never stored.
