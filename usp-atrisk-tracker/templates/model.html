In plain words

  • •The system has learned from {{ '{:,}'.format(s.n_students) }} past students in {{ s.n_courses }} courses whose final results are known.
  • •For each of your students it looks at the marks so far, such as their average, how they compare with the class, missed work and whether marks are rising or falling, and finds how similar past students finished.
  • •"60% chance of failing" means that of 100 similar past students at the same point, about 60 failed. It is a prompt to check in, not a verdict.
  • •It works for any course, whatever its assessments are called or weighted. A course with no past results gets a simpler estimate, clearly labelled.
