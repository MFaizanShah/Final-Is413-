What it looks for

  • A table of assessments with a weight or percentage column, for example Assessment | Weight | Out of | Due. Headings like Component, Task, Item, Percentage, Weighting or Worth work too.
  • If there is no table, lines such as Mid Semester Test ...... 20%.
  • The course code (like IS413) and title from the heading or a "Course code" row, and the semester and campus where given.
  • Scanned PDFs that are only pictures cannot be read; upload the Word version or type the assessments in.
